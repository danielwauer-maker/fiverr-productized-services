## 3. Gig Description

Does your Power BI report work, but you are not sure whether the model, DAX, performance or dashboard structure is truly solid?

I will perform a professional Power BI health assessment and give you a clear, prioritized view of what is working, what creates risk and what you should improve next.

Depending on the package, I review:
• Data model architecture and relationships
• DAX quality and maintainability
• Power Query and transformation logic
• Performance risks
• Dashboard UX and visual structure
• KPI and business alignment
• Maintainability and governance

You receive a professional PDF assessment with a Health Score, confirmed findings, priorities, quick wins and actionable recommendations.

This Gig is an expert audit and optimization plan — not a full dashboard rebuild. That keeps the scope focused and gives you an independent second opinion before investing time in implementation.

For large or enterprise models, please contact me before ordering so I can confirm the right scope.

## 4. FAQs

**What exactly will you review?**  
Depending on your package, I review the semantic model, relationships, DAX, Power Query, performance risks, report structure, dashboard UX, KPI alignment and maintainability. The Standard and Premium packages provide the complete Health Score framework.

**Will you fix all issues in my Power BI file?**  
No. This Gig is designed as an independent expert audit and optimization plan. I identify and prioritize issues and explain the recommended solution. Implementation can be scoped separately when needed.

**What is included in a revision?**  
One revision covers factual corrections or clarification of the delivered assessment. It does not include a new audit after you modify the report or model. A changed file requires a separate re-validation.

**Can you review a PBIX file?**  
Yes. PBIX files are accepted. For structured static analysis I may create a PBIP working copy locally from the provided file. Your original file is not modified as part of the audit.

**Is my data kept confidential?**  
I treat submitted project files and business information as confidential and use them only to complete the ordered service. Please remove credentials, passwords, secrets or unnecessary personal data before uploading.

**My model is larger than your package limits. Can you still review it?**  
Yes. Please contact me before ordering. I will confirm the scope and provide a custom offer for larger or enterprise-scale models.

**Can you focus only on DAX, performance or the data model?**  
Yes. The Basic package is designed for one clearly defined focus area or problem. For a full review across all areas, choose Standard or Premium.

**What makes the Premium package different?**  
Premium adds a deeper technical review and a prioritized Optimization Blueprint. It is designed for clients who need not only findings, but also a structured sequence for remediation and implementation planning.

**Do you need access to my Power BI Service or Microsoft account?**  
Usually no. The audit is primarily file-based. If a specific issue depends on service configuration, I will tell you exactly what additional evidence is required. Never send passwords or authentication secrets.

**Can you review a report before I invest in a larger redesign or development project?**  
Yes. That is one of the main use cases for this service. The assessment gives you an independent view of technical quality, risks, quick wins and priorities before committing to implementation work.
