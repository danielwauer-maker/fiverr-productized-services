# Fiverr Hero Gig Graphics System V1

## Purpose
Create a premium Fiverr gallery that communicates expertise in under three seconds and looks closer to a boutique BI consultancy than a low-cost freelance listing.

## Canvas
- 1280 × 769 px
- PNG
- Keep critical text inside generous safe margins
- No final umbrella brand until naming is frozen

## Visual tokens
- Deep Navy #0B1F33
- Interaction Blue #2F6BFF
- Success #1E9E63
- Warning #E39A22
- Critical #D64545
- Background #F5F7FA

- Never claim customer outcomes from the demo case.

- No fake star ratings, buyer counts, “#1”, or unsupported claims.
- Keep Fiverr gallery images brand-neutral until the umbrella name is finalized.
